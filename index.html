THE OVARIAN HEALTH GARDEN

Knowledge is power.

A few things worth knowing, without the medical jargon.

✿

Ovarian cysts

Fluid-filled sacs on or within an ovary are common. Many are harmless and resolve, but some require assessment or follow-up.

❀

When pain is urgent

Sudden severe one-sided pelvic pain, particularly with nausea or vomiting, can indicate ovarian torsion or another emergency.

✾

Cysts vs tumours

A cyst is not automatically cancer. Symptoms alone cannot distinguish benign cysts, tumours and other pelvic conditions.

✳

What doctors may check

Depending on symptoms, clinicians may consider a pregnancy test, examination, blood tests and pelvic ultrasound.
